## 1. Critical Reassessment of the Provisional 20 Problem Families

The provisional *Goal & Capability Reference (v1.0)* enumerates 20 RNA-seq problem families, mirrored in [`src/devbench/core/rna_answerability.py`](../../../src/devbench/core/rna_answerability.py) (20 families, 39 registered question contracts, 1,177 candidate rows in [`execution-release-gate.md`](../../../docs/audits/2026-11-23-rna-capabilities/execution-release-gate.md)). Evaluating that flat taxonomy against modern transcriptomics consensus—*Best practices for single-cell analysis across modalities* (Heumos et al., *Nat. Rev. Genet.* 2023), *Orchestrating Single-Cell Analysis with Bioconductor* (OSCA; Amezquita et al., *Nat. Methods* 2020), the **scverse** core data standards (`AnnData`, `MuData`, `SpatialData`), and `nf-core` production pipelines—reveals **two structural defects**: category conflation and six missing scientific preconditions.

### 1.1 Structural Critique: Four Conflated Categories
A flat 1–20 list forces incompatible concepts into sibling slots:

1. **Biological inference estimands** (`#1` DE/differential state, `#2` cell identity/heterogeneity, `#3` abundance/composition/deconvolution, `#4` splicing/DTU, `#6` pathways/programs/networks, `#7` trajectories/transitions, `#8` integration/comparative, `#10` cell–cell communication, `#11` perturbation, `#12` genetic regulation/ASE, `#18` phenotype prediction).
2. **Assay modalities, platforms & transcript biotypes** (`#5` long-read/annotation, `#9` spatial, `#14` small/noncoding/circular/repetitive RNAs, `#15` nascent/turnover/modification/translation, `#16` host–pathogen/community, `#17` multimodal/repertoire/clonal). In real investigations these **cross-multiply** with inference estimands (e.g., spatial differential expression, long-read single-cell isoform usage, CITE-seq differential abundance).
3. **Disease-domain composites** (`#13` cancer transcript abnormalities): matched tumor–normal DE belongs to `#1`, aberrant splicing to `#4`, expressed variants/ASE to `#12`, and clonal phylogeny to `#17`; only somatic RNA fusions, expressed somatic variants, RNA editing, and inferred somatic CNVs constitute distinct assay-coupled endpoints.
4. **Cross-cutting meta-scientific governance** (`#19` reproduction/replication/synthesis, `#20` experimental design & method development): these govern *every* biological family rather than competing alongside them.

Furthermore, a closed enumeration of 20 families creates an anti-research trap: when a thesis researcher poses a novel cross-cutting or assay-specific question (e.g., splicing-coupled nonsense-mediated decay along an immune differentiation gradient), a closed answerability router either refuses falsely or shoehorns the question into the wrong solver.

### 1.2 Six Critical Blind Spots Missing from Provisional v1.0 (`M1`–`M6`)

## 2. Orthogonal 3-Axis Capability Model

To preserve complete traceability to provisional v1.0 while eliminating category conflation and enabling open-ended research, the agent's problem space is structured into **three orthogonal axes** (`Axis A × Axis B`, governed by `Axis C`), executed across the non-linear Epistemic Investigation Graph (`E0`–`E6` + `E_ABSTAIN`) defined in [03-adaptive-research-design.md](03-adaptive-research-design.md).

### 2.1 Axis A — Assay, Data Custody & Forensic Preconditions (`A0`–`A3`)
Axis A declares *what physical measurements exist*, *how raw counts and coordinates are stored out-of-core*, and *whether unit identity and artifact profiles support inference*.

- **`A0` — Upstream Data Preparation & Out-of-Core Storage Custody:**
  - Standardized alignment/quantification interfaces (`nf-core/rnaseq`, `nf-core/scrnaseq`, long-read `Bambu`/`IsoQuant`, bulk `Salmon`/`tximport`) and immutable read-only custody over `COUNT-1` raw counts.
  - Out-of-core container adapters: `AnnData` (H5AD / Zarr / Dask), `MuData` (`.h5mu`), `SpatialData` (OME-NGFF Zarr + GeoParquet), `TileDB-SOMA`, and streaming Apache Arrow / Parquet tables.
- **`A1` — Donor/Sample Forensics & Genetic Demultiplexing [NEW]:**
  - Automated pre-inference verification of sample/donor identity: pooled-lane genetic demultiplexing (`souporcell`, `Vireo`, `demuxlet`), genotype-to-RNA swap/contamination checks (`VerifyBamID2`, `QTLtools MBV`), sex-chromosome expression consistency (`XIST`/`UTY`/`RPS4Y1`), and cross-sample correlation/duplicate forensic screens.
  - Hard rule: forensic mismatch halts confirmatory biological-unit inference (`E_ABSTAIN` or back-edge to `E1`) until resolved.
- **`A2` — Modality-Aware QC & Artifact Dispositions:**
  - Assay-specific QC profiles: whole-cell scRNA-seq (mtRNA, UMI/gene complexity, per-lane `scDblFinder`, `EmptyDrops`, ambient `CellBender`/`SoupX`/`DecontX`); snRNA-seq (nuclear/intronic fraction via `DropletQC`, relaxed/optional mtRNA, neuronal/tissue ambient spillover sensitivity); bulk RNA-seq (mapping/duplication/3'-bias/rRNA, library-size outliers); spatial (tissue-boundary masks, capture-depth spatial autocorrelation audit).
  - Mandatory **differential QC-retention report** across donors, captures, conditions, and candidate cell populations so QC does not silently drop a biological state or create differential bias.
- **`A3` — Modality & Biotype Qualifiers:**
  - Typed assay modifiers attached to any `Axis B` estimand: `bulk_short_read`, `scrna_whole_cell`, `snrna_nuclei`, `long_read_bulk_or_sc`, `spatial_sequencing_or_imaging`, `small_nc_circ_repeat_rna`, `nascent_kinetics_mod_riboseq`, `host_pathogen_community`, `multimodal_cite_multiome_repertoire`.

### 2.2 Axis B — Core Biological Inference Classes (`B1`–`B12` + `B_OPEN`)
Every research aim declares a primary estimand in `B1`–`B12` or explicitly enters `B_OPEN` when the biological question spans novel or unlisted statistical targets.

| Axis B ID | Inference Class | Core Scientific Estimand(s) | Mandatory Anti-Circularity / Unit Invariants |
|---|---|---|---|
| **B1** | **Replicated Differential Expression & Differential State** | Donor/biological-unit condition, interaction, or continuous-covariate effects in bulk or within-population scRNA/snRNA pseudobulk & mixed models. | `COUNT-1` raw counts (or verified `tximport` + effective lengths); biological unit = donor/subject (never cells/tech reps); rank/confounding check; effect intervals + leave-one-donor-out (LODO) sensitivity. |
| **B2** | **Cell Identity, Heterogeneity, Rare Populations & Selective Marker Inference** | Clustering, rare-population discovery, reference-guided cell annotation with uncertainty, and post-clustering marker testing. | Strict separation between descriptive marker rankings (`E2` exploratory, no confirmatory p/q) and selective/split marker inference (`ClusterDE`, `CADET`, dispersion-verified `countsplit`) or orthogonal-label validation (`E5`). |
| **B3** | **Cell Abundance, Compositional Shifts, Neighborhood DA & Deconvolution** | Relative cell-type proportion shifts (`scCODA`/`tascCODA`/`propeller`), cluster-free kNN neighborhood DA (`miloR`/`pertpy.tools.Milo`), and bulk cell-fraction deconvolution. | Explicit compositional closure disclosure (relative vs absolute density); donor-level covariates/pairing; reference-type sensitivity & multi-chain MCMC convergence; missing-reference-type sensitivity for deconvolution. |
| **B4** | **Alternative Splicing, Differential Transcript Usage & Isoform Discovery** | Replicate-aware differential transcript/exon usage (DTU/DEU), junction/LSV splicing ($\Delta\Psi$), and long-read isoform reconstruction & structural curation. | Donor-level overdispersion modeling resilient to unequal sequencing depth; stage-wise gene/transcript OFDR (`stageR`); orthogonal support (short-read/CAGE/polyA/`SQANTI3`) required before claiming novel isoforms. |
| **B5** | **Gene Programs, Pathways, Regulons & Regulatory Networks** | Tested-universe ORA, stat-ranked GSEA (`fgsea`), competitive inter-gene-correlated tests (`CAMERA`), consensus TF/pathway activity (`decoupleR`), de novo programs (NMF/cNMF), enhancer-driven GRNs (`SCENIC+`). | Tested-universe receipt pinned from upstream DE for ALL ORA/regulon/motif calls; explicit distinction between coexpression association, curated TF-target activity, motif presence, and causal physical binding. |
| **B6** | **Temporal Dynamics, Trajectories & State Transitions** | Observed collection-time donor splines, multi-sample differential trajectories (`Lamian`/`condiments`), multi-view fate probabilities (`CellRank 2`/`Palantir`), and exploratory RNA velocity. | Pseudotime ≠ chronological time; confirmatory trajectory DE requires independently supported axis or selection-aware testing; RNA velocity strictly `E2` exploratory without metabolic labeling / time-series / lineage tracing. |
| **B7** | **Integration, Atlas Query-to-Reference Mapping & Cross-Species Comparison** | Multi-batch/multi-study latent integration, query-to-reference transfer (`scArches`/`Azimuth`/`scPoli`), and cross-species cell-type alignment (`SAMap`/`SATURN`). | Dual-axis `scib-metrics` (batch removal AND bio-conservation); never enforce single-component topology on disjoint cell types; explicit paralog/orthology policy for cross-species. |
| **B8** | **Spatial Tissue Organization, Domains, SVGs & Spot/Cell Mapping** | Tissue-masked spatial graphs, spatially variable genes (SVGs), tissue domain segmentation (`BANKSY`), spot deconvolution (`RCTD`/`Cell2location`), and multi-donor spatial comparisons. | Radius/Delaunay + tissue-mask graphs (no cross-gap KNN bridging); mandatory unadjusted vs capture-depth-conditioned SVG sensitivity (Bhuva et al., 2024); multi-section/donor replication for treatment claims. |
| **B9** | **Cell–Cell Communication & Niche Signaling** | Curated ligand–receptor complex coexpression (`scverse/liana`), donor-stratified differential signaling, and downstream receiver target-gene activity (`MultiNicheNet`). | Donor-level replication (not cell-pair inflation); curated complex subunit stoichiometry; coexpression never equals physical contact or causal signaling without spatial/perturbation support. |
| **B10** | **Perturbation Responses & Causal Functional Genomics** | Low/high-MOI single-cell CRISPR screen DE (`SCEPTRE`), perturbation efficacy/escapee filtering (`Mixscape`/`pertpy`), and confounded counterfactual matching (`CINEMA-OT`). | Negative-control (non-targeting gRNA) empirical calibration; randomization unit & gRNA assignment fidelity; linear-baseline comparator gate before any deep perturbation predictor (Ahlmann-Eltze et al., 2025). |
| **B11** | **Genetic Regulation, eQTLs, Allelic Expression & Somatic Transcript Abnormalities** | Bulk & pseudobulk cis/trans eQTL (`tensorQTL`), allele-specific expression with mapping-bias control (`WASP`), and somatic cancer transcriptomics (fusions, RNA editing, expressed variants, inferred CNVs). | Matched genotype/RNA forensics (`A1`); reference-mapping bias correction (`WASP`); tumor-vs-normal expression DE routed cleanly to `B1` rather than duplicated here. |
| **B12** | **Predictive Modeling, Signatures & Transportable Phenotype Associations** | Supervised diagnostic/prognostic/phenotype classification, regression, and survival signatures with out-of-distribution calibration. | Strict donor/cohort/site-grouped nested CV; training-fold-only preprocessing & feature selection; `PartitionVault` holdout embargo; calibration slope/Brier + external transportability disclosure. |
| **B_OPEN** | **Open / Unclassified / Novel Estimands [EXPLICIT LANE]** | Arbitrary thesis-grade RNA-seq questions that do not map cleanly to `B1`–`B12` (e.g., novel composite statistics, cross-modal mechanistic tests, custom assay QC/modeling). | Executes in **Tier 2 Governed Exploratory Sandbox** (`E2`) with full data custody (`A0`–`A2`); may promote to confirmatory (`E5`) ONLY by passing explicit unit-accounting, negative-control calibration (`E4`), and Independent Critic review ([03-adaptive-research-design.md](03-adaptive-research-design.md), [04-system-architecture-and-governance.md](04-system-architecture-and-governance.md)). |

### 2.3 Axis C — Meta-Scientific Lifecycle & Verification Governance (`C1`–`C2`)
- **`C1` — Reproduction, External Replication & Evidence Synthesis:** Bitwise/statistical replay of published protocols, cross-cohort meta-analysis (random-effects / concordance of effect directions, not vote-counting p-values), contradictory-evidence preservation, and citation-grounded literature custody.
- **`C2` — Experimental Design, Power/Identifiability & Method Benchmarking:** A priori contrast estimability, sample-size/power simulation under donor-level variance, and fair method-comparison campaigns (freezing comparison criteria before viewing results, requiring simple/classical baselines, and enforcing parameter-attribution provenance separating user-prespecified vs default/agent-proposed choices).

## 3. Priority-Stratified Computational Method Specifications

### 3.1 Priority 1: Single-Cell & Single-Nucleus RNA-seq (`scRNA-seq` / `snRNA-seq`)

1. **Droplet Calling, Ambient Decontamination, Nuclear QC & Doublets (`A2`)**:
   - **Empty droplets & ambient RNA**: Retain `EmptyDrops` (Lun et al., *Genome Biol.* 2019) for raw unfiltered droplet matrices and `CellBender` (Fleming et al., *Nat. Methods* 2023; **strictly pin `>=0.3.2` — locked at `0.4.0` in [`containers/rna-kernel-2.0/python-cellbender.lock`](../../../containers/rna-kernel-2.0/python-cellbender.lock)** because `v0.3.1` was yanked for an output-count integer overflow bug), supplemented by `SoupX` (Young & Behjati, *GigaScience* 2020) and `DecontX` (Yang et al., *Genome Biol.* 2020). Per **`ADR-27` (`UD-13` / `Q2.4`)**, route deterministically across `(input provenance R0 vs R1) x (CUDA vs CPU)`:
     - `r0_cellbender`: `R0` raw droplets + `CUDA` GPU primary (or bounded CPU: `<= 25,000` raw droplets, `<= 4` captures, explicit `--allow-cellbender-cpu`, max 1 retry at `0.5x` learning rate before fallback);
     - `r0_cpu_soupx_decontx`: `R0` raw droplets + CPU-only (including macOS Docker/Podman) or over-budget (`SoupX` for scRNA; `decontX(..., background = raw_droplets)` for snRNA or when `SoupX::autoEstCont()` warns/fails, recording `cellbender_deferred_reason`);
     - `r1_filtered_only_decontx_heuristic`: `R1` filtered counts only (`CellBender` and `SoupX` fail closed with `missing_raw_droplet_matrix`; filtered-only `DecontX` is admitted solely as `E3` exploratory sensitivity).
     Keep raw `R1` `COUNT-1` and `R2` ambient-corrected matrices as distinct linked layers: run primary pseudobulk DE on raw `R1` `COUNT-1` AND `R2` ambient-adjusted sensitivity in `E3` (Gate 2, mandatory when ambient burden shifts across conditions or in brain/solid-tissue snRNA-seq per Caglayan et al., *Neuron* 2022), logging `ambient_method`, `decontamination_regime`, `input_matrix_tier`, `hardware_backend`, `cellbender_version`, and `cellbender_deferred_reason`.
   - **snRNA-seq nuclear & intronic QC**: Incorporate `DropletQC` (Muskovic & Powell, *Genome Biol.* 2021) nuclear fraction (`intronic / (intronic + exonic)`) when BAM/unspliced counts are present to distinguish intact nuclei from damaged cells and empty droplets, removing the whole-cell mitochondrial bias identified in Slyper et al. (*Nat. Med.* 2020) while preserving PR #13's graceful `NaN` handling (`singlecell.py:73-75`) when mito symbols are absent.
   - **Per-capture doublet detection**: Replace Scrublet-only fixed 5% default (`Wolock et al., 2019`) with `scDblFinder` (Germain et al., *F1000Research* 2022, active Bioconductor 2025–2026) executed per capture/lane with capture-specific multiplet expectations; retain Scrublet as secondary Python-only fallback. Report differential doublet and QC-removal rates across donors, conditions, and clusters.
2. **Donor/Sample Forensics & Genetic Demultiplexing (`A1`)**:
   - When BAMs/barcodes or SNP VCFs are present, execute genotype-free (`souporcell`, Heaton et al., *Nat. Methods* 2020) or genotype-guided (`Vireo`, Huang et al., *Genome Biol.* 2019; `demuxlet`, Kang et al., *Nat. Biotechnol.* 2018) demultiplexing to verify genetic donor identity, inter-donor doublets, and sample swaps. When only expression matrices are available, run mandatory expression-level sex-chromosome concordance (`XIST`/`UTY`/`RPS4Y1`) and donor-pseudobulk identity checks, logging explicitly that *genotype-free expression checks cannot prove donor independence* (`L1` forensic caveat).
3. **Normalization & Representation Selection (`A2` / `E1`)**:
   - Follow Ahlmann-Eltze & Huber (*Nat. Methods* 2023), who demonstrated across 22 datasets that **shifted logarithm** (`log1p(y / s)`) with size factors (`scran` deconvolution size factors, Lun et al., *Genome Biol.* 2016) matches or outperforms complex variance-stabilizing / analytic Pearson residual transforms for PCA/kNN graph construction. ### 5.2 Five Architectural & Evaluation Imperatives Distilled for This Specification
1. **Fixed small-tool menus choke open-ended research (`Biomni`, `scBench`):** A 15-operation discriminated `Plan` union cannot anticipate non-10x chemistries (`scBench` 40+ pp drop), bespoke longitudinal formulas, or novel `B_OPEN` questions. Dynamic code execution over a curated Python/R/Bioconductor environment (`Biomni` / `OpenHands` pattern) is mandatory for generality.
2. **Unguarded free-form code silently destroys statistical validity (`BioAgent Bench`, DevHarness probes):** Arbitrary code exits `0` while committing pseudoreplication, double-dipping, uncalibrated DTU, inflated ORA backgrounds (`0.018 -> 6.02e-9`), depth-confounded spatial SVGs, or running on decoy/corrupted files.
3. **Dual-Lane Governed Execution resolves the dilemma ([04-system-architecture-and-governance.md](04-system-architecture-and-governance.md)):** Combine **Tier 1 Curated Scientific Kernels** (digest-pinned, `L2`-calibrated biostatistics engines) with a **Tier 2 Governed Exploratory Code Sandbox** (`--network none`, RO vault mounts, automatic hashing) whose outputs are cryptographically tagged `E2` exploratory and cannot enter `E5` confirmatory claims without passing custody, unit, calibration, and Critic gates.
4. **Separate Author from Independent Critic/Verifier (`Virtual Lab`, `Agent Laboratory`):** Author self-evaluation (`CellAgent`) optimizes internal heuristics; a non-author Critic checking arithmetic/BH/universe integrity, LODO/sensitivity stability, plot-data sidecar consistency, and epistemic-tier language prevents over-claiming.
5. **Calibrate abstention symmetrically & separate provenance from vision claims (`BixBench`, `CORE-Bench`):** Because frontier LLMs over-refuse when given an opt-out (`BixBench`), evaluation MUST pair unanswerable traps with closely matched answerable edge cases ([06-milestones-and-evaluation.md](06-milestones-and-evaluation.md)). Plot sidecars are mandated for **numerical-figure provenance**, not because of a nonexistent BixBench Fig. 5 vision-accuracy penalty.

## 7. Concrete Acceptance Criteria (Problem Space & Method Contracts)

1. **Orthogonal Registry & Open-Lane Continuity:** `src/devbench/core/rna_answerability.py` encodes `Axis A (A0–A3) × Axis B (B1–B12 + B_OPEN) × Axis C (C1–C2)` while retaining deterministic 1-to-1 lookup metadata back to legacy v1.0 Families `#1`–`#20`; novel valid questions route to `B_OPEN` (Tier 2 exploratory) instead of falsely returning `unsupported_family`.
2. **Forensic & Modality Precondition Enforcement:** Intake rejects or downgrades confirmatory biological-unit inference when `A1` forensic checks detect a donor/sex/genotype swap or when an integer-valued `log2(1+x)` synthetic probe is falsely declared `COUNT-1`; snRNA-seq inputs without mitochondrial symbols run cleanly with nuclear/intronic `DropletQC` metrics when unspliced/BAM inputs exist.
3. **Priority 1 scRNA/snRNA Method Parity & Replacement:** All P1 replacements (`scDblFinder`, `scib-metrics`, `miloR` primary / `pertpy.tools.Milo` `E4` [`ADR-26`], `CellBender 0.4.0`/`SoupX`/`DecontX` [`ADR-27`], `Ensembl+in-paralogs`/`SAMap`/`SATURN` [`ADR-28`], multi-chain `scCODA`, `ClusterDE`/`CADET`, `dreamlet`, `scverse/liana`, `SCEPTRE`, `CellRank 2`) execute via digest-pinned Tier 1 containers with verified receipts, effect intervals, and LODO diagnostics where applicable, and zero P1 solvers remain unwired from the 13-tool MCP control plane.
4. **Statistical Calibration & Numerical Equivalence Before Confirmatory Promotion:** Neither `edgeR v4` / `limma-voom` / `dreamlet` pseudobulk/bulk DE (following the pre-specified, label-blind `ADR-15` regime-routing policy), DTU (`bulk_dtu.py` vs `satuRn`/`DRIMSeq`+`stageR`), post-clustering markers (`ClusterDE`/`CADET`/dispersion-verified `countsplit`), nor depth-conditioned spatial SVGs (`SPARK-X`) may emit `L2`-qualified confirmatory receipts until passing their respective null-FDR, depth-imbalance, comparator, and `ADR-16` cross-architecture numerical-equivalence gates defined in [06-milestones-and-evaluation.md](06-milestones-and-evaluation.md).
5. **Anti-Hype Comparator Gate:** Any analysis invoking a single-cell foundation model (`scGPT`/`Geneformer`), deep perturbation predictor, or RNA velocity MUST fail closed on confirmatory promotion unless accompanied by an executed `E4` comparison against frozen classical/linear baselines or orthogonal experimental corroboration.
