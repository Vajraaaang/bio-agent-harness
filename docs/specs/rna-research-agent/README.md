## 1. Revised Overarching Thesis-Grade Research Goal

Build a general, local-first, governed **Automated RNA-seq Research Agent** (`devharness` / `DevBench`) capable of planning, executing, falsifying, iterating, and synthesizing **sustained, multi-aim, thesis-grade transcriptomics investigations** — prioritizing `scRNA-seq` and `snRNA-seq` (`P1`), hardening bulk RNA-seq (`P2`), and preserving full generality across emerging assays and novel biological questions (`P3`).

Informed by the repository baseline audit ([01-baseline-audit-and-gaps.md](01-baseline-audit-and-gaps.md)), primary transcriptomics and autonomous bioinformatics agent literature ([02-problem-space-and-methods.md](02-problem-space-and-methods.md)), and live-verified Codex/MCP specifications ([05-codex-and-mcp-integration.md](05-codex-and-mcp-integration.md)), the revised specification updates the provisional v1.0 goal along five foundational principles:

1. **Generality Without Sacrificing Rigor:** Replace rigid fixed-operation menus (which choke open-ended science and cause 40+ percentage-point drops on non-10x technologies in `scBench`) *and* reject unguarded free-form bash execution (which exits `0` while committing pseudoreplication, double-dipping, background inflation, or silent failures on corrupted inputs in `BioAgent Bench`). Combine **Tier 1 Curated Calibrated Kernels** with a **Tier 2 Governed Exploratory Code Sandbox** under one local custody authority.
2. **Orthogonal Capability & Open-Question Coverage:** Organize RNA-seq problem space along three orthogonal axes — **Assay/Custody/Forensics (`Axis A: A0–A3`)**, **Core Biological Inference (`Axis B: B1–B12` + explicit `B_OPEN` unclassified lane)**, and **Meta-Scientific Governance (`Axis C: C1–C2`)** — closing six critical v1.0 blind spots (donor/sample forensics, snRNA nuclear/ambient QC, post-clustering selective inference, cross-species homology, single-cell foundation model vs. classical baseline gates, and out-of-core multi-GB storage).
3. **Non-Linear Epistemic Progression Over Immutable Custody:** Decouple append-only artifact custody (SQLite WAL + SHA-256 DAG) from a non-linear **Epistemic Investigation Graph (`E0`–`E6` + `E_ABSTAIN`)** supporting parallel competing branches, mid-session external input admission, executable falsification probes, transitive dependency invalidation, calibrated abstention (preventing `BixBench`-style over-refusal), and non-author Independent Critic verification.
4. **Strict Four-Level Claim Separation (`L1`–`L4`):** Never conflate software execution (`L1_execution_verified`), statistical error-rate control (`L2_statistically_calibrated`, including automated post-fit falsification + `ai_critic_verified: true`), independently corroborated or human-panel-adjudicated biological validity (`L3_scientifically_supported`, and platform-level `M5` Scorecards 2–6), and regulated medical deployment (`L4`: permanently held, `clinical_release_allowed = false`).
5. **Reproducible 7-Deliverable Thesis Research Package:** Every sustained investigation seals a replayable, hash-verified research dossier capturing questions/aims/literature, data/forensics/pinned references, executable code/containers/seeds/DAG, separated `COUNT-1` raw vs. derived representations, complete numeric tables + paired plot sidecars, versioned methods/diagnostics/Critic reviews, and transparent results/negative findings/failed attempts/limitations/follow-up experiments.
