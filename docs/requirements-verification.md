# Clinical requirements and implementation evidence

The [0.4.17 primary audit integration](../reports/code-action-audit/README.md), [0.4.16 runtime metadata and Colab verification](../reports/runtime-identity/README.md), [0.4.15 audit and Colab verification](../reports/audit-verification/README.md), [0.4.14 observed-temporal checks](../reports/temporal-verification/README.md), [0.4.13 input-preservation checkpoint](../reports/input-preservation/README.md) and separately executed [0.4.12 Colab checks](../reports/colab-verification/README.md) add software evidence. Clinical qualification remains unestablished.

The implementation is research software with explicit clinical admission and release holds. Version 0.4.12 adds experimental Gaussian selection Monte Carlo inference and rejects numerically unresolved Gaussian fits. Its full local suite passed 1,908 tests with two platform skips; 32 installed-wheel checks and a separately frozen API diagnostic/replay also passed. See the [public verification summary](../reports/api-verification/README.md). These results do not qualify a biomarker, validate a clinical assay or certify regulatory compliance.

The authoritative development records are versioned result directories. Test counts, cryptographic receipts and simulated performance have different meanings and must remain separate. The [current clinical-production goal](clinical-production-goal.md), [clinical assessment of the earlier baseline](clinical-readiness.md) and [Part 11 control map](part11-control-map.md) provide the surrounding requirements. Earlier claims in this file are preserved in `results/trajectory-invariant-2026-09-08/before/docs/requirements-verification.md`.

## Evidence that contradicts a readiness claim

The 4,000-cohort endpoint-interval study retained 3,990 usable results and ten timing-verification failures. In the higher-dispersion setting, 1,868/2,000 selected effects were covered: 93.4%, with a pointwise 95% interval of 92.22–94.45%. That study triggered its prespecified undercoverage warning. These outcomes remain adverse evidence; later software repairs and unused diagnostic flags cannot convert them into acceptance.

The separate reference-normalization comparison attempted 1,024 arms over 512 fresh cohorts. It produced 986 numerical completions and 38 higher-dispersion zero-reference refusals. Mean signed error moved toward zero while paired mean squared error and interval width increased. The higher-dispersion comparison uses 218 common completions, not unequal arm denominators, and does not establish improved coverage.

The 0.4.10 temporal correction cannot clear either calibration result. Those studies use multiple timepoints and different inference machinery. Its supported claim is narrower: absent transitions and malformed estimates no longer receive successful temporal evidence, and compact summaries retain distinct numeric time identities.

## LatchBio reference and regulatory interpretation

LatchBio documents workflow versions derived from a chosen version string and directory hash, with Git commit/working-tree information when available. Its organization settings expose login-provider and email-domain restrictions. These are useful reference capabilities for version identity and administered access; they are not evidence that this repository or a particular biomarker assay is qualified.[^1][^2]

This repository has packaged source and content-hash evidence, but no Git HEAD or governed release remote was present at the current inspection. Its configured keys and policy checks require a real independently administered identity and approval process before regulated attribution can be claimed. No production provider or clinical owner is substituted by fixture identities.

FDA biomarker qualification concerns a stated context of use and reliable measurement supporting that use. It does not itself clear or approve the particular testing device for patient care. The intended regulatory route therefore remains a required project decision rather than a property inferred from the word “biomarker.”[^3]

FDA's clinical electronic-systems guidance describes validation suited to intended use and risk, alongside record, access and signature controls. Package installation, unit tests and content hashes contribute software evidence but do not supply the complete organizational validation and operating record.[^4]

## Decisions and evidence needed for clinical admission

The next qualification protocol requires the biomarker or composite score, specimen/assay, intended population, clinical or drug-development decision, applicable route and record owner. It also requires the deployment owner, identity provider, retention/restore responsibilities and independent quality authority. Representative data access and its allowed processing boundary must be approved before clinical inputs are admitted.

A statistical analysis plan then needs a locked estimand, hypothesis family, preprocessing and normalization/reference policy, batch and biological-unit model, missingness/refusal handling, selection procedure and acceptance margins. Acceptance must be declared before the corresponding held-out qualification evidence is evaluated. The existing synthetic scenarios should inform risk and design, not supply invented clinical limits.

The governing decision remains **clinical admission and release unavailable**. Continuing research development is appropriate within the documented input and execution boundaries; neither this map nor a complete set of locally passing tests changes that decision.
