# Scientific Capability Audit Report

**RNA-seq Workbench · 23 November 2026 · Revision `ad1d74724e1e4d12a3c0fe6840e6521bd34a445b`**

**Material reviewed.** The implementation, its scientific contracts and reference playbooks, public-method papers, synthetic tests, and a retained public airway audit. The numerical studies below use invented donors, counts, or coordinates. The airway record covers eight libraries from four donor-derived cell lines.

**Decision.** Can a researcher use this platform to support a thesis, and can its outputs by themselves establish a publication-grade conclusion? The first requires executable methods appropriate to the study, protection of the experimental unit, interpretable effects, and auditable failures. The second also requires calibrated inference and independent evidence for the intended assay, population and claim. These are the audit’s criteria. The 7.5% null-FDR ceiling below comes from the repository’s pre-existing frozen evaluation. [Assessment definitions][matrix] · [Frozen evaluation design][frozen-design]

**Verdict. A researcher can use the platform for a supervised thesis with a bounded question. General-purpose autonomous publication-grade inference falls short of the threshold.** It executes replicated count models, tests relative cell abundance, separates marker discovery from testing in a dedicated route, and can verify the gene background for enrichment. Two findings control the verdict. The primary differential-expression workflow missed its frozen false-discovery criterion in three of five designs. The transcript-usage estimator produced a large, reproducible false-positive excess under unequal donor depth. Both completed normally. A paper should identify the exact workflow, state the experimental unit, and supply the independent or sensitivity evidence needed for that particular conclusion. [Calibration results](evidence.json) · [Reproducible stress tests](reproduce_probes.py)

## What the numerical checks showed

### Frozen differential-expression challenge

The mounted ARM64 run used R 4.6.1 and edgeR 4.10.7. Each design had 200 complete-null and 200 separately generated planted cohorts, 192 genes per cohort, and 20 expression profiles. The independent designs used 20 donors. The paired designs used ten. For the single-cell designs, simulated cell counts were summed back into donor profiles. The positive panel had 24 genes with signed threefold changes per cohort. BH was applied at 5%. With every null true, a cohort with at least one discovery has false discovery proportion one, so the fraction of such cohorts estimates FDR. [Generator and thresholds][frozen-design] · [Independent BH check and scoring][calculation]

| Design | Null cohorts with discoveries | Estimated FDR, exact 95% interval | Planted genes recovered out of 4,800 | Frozen 7.5% ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Independent bulk | 18/200 | 9.0% (5.42–13.85%) | 4,782 | Exceeded |
| Paired bulk | 13/200 | 6.5% (3.51–10.86%) | 4,800 | Met |
| Independent single-cell pseudobulk | 21/200 | 10.5% (6.62–15.60%) | 4,784 | Exceeded |

| Two populations paired within donor | 9/200 | 4.5% (2.08–8.37%) | 4,800 | Met |

All 2,000 fits finished, no discovered planted gene had a reversed effect direction, and 200 additional completely confounded cohorts were refused. The point-estimate acceptance rule failed. Every interval includes 7.5%, which limits precision about the underlying rates. The planted panel establishes high sensitivity to large effects under this generator. Power for small effects remains unknown. A documented limma-voom comparison improved independent-design null performance at the cost of moderate-effect bulk power, so a blanket solver switch has insufficient support. ### A direct failure under donor-depth imbalance

In each of 250 cohorts, six donors per arm drew transcript fractions independently from the same Beta(25,25) distribution. Binomial sampling generated counts for two complementary transcripts of one gene. The fitted condition term had no true effect. We called the current core per-transcript estimator. At 100 reads per donor, its grouped quasi-binomial test rejected **16/250** at nominal 5% (6.4%, exact 95% interval 3.70–10.19%). With the first donor in each arm sequenced to 10,000 reads and all others at 100, it rejected **184/250** (73.6%, 67.68–78.95%). Every fit returned a finite probability. Seed 337 was reset for each regime. [Audit reproduction](reproduce_probes.py) · [Estimator][dtu-fit] · [Two-feature screening rule][dtu-screen]

This measures marginal type-I error for one null gene. With exactly two complementary transcripts, the two tests coincide and the gene screen uses that single unique test. Read depth gives a small number of donors disproportionate leverage when donor usage varies. Stage-wise adjustment operates on the resulting p-values and cannot restore their calibration. **Withhold confirmatory differential transcript usage for heterogeneous donor depth** until a donor-overdispersion model passes independent depth and leverage stress tests. DRIMSeq and stageR supply distinct comparators for the count model and the downstream error criterion. [Nowicka & Robinson](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.8900.2) · [Van den Berge et al.](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-017-1277-0)

## Module findings and scientific vulnerabilities

### Answerability, measurements and provenance

The registry asks which signal the assay physically measured, checks linked axes, and requires design and runtime evidence before promoting a question. It can return a request for information, a scientific refusal, a descriptive observation, or an exploratory extension. Estimated bulk counts require matching effective lengths. The 20-family matrix tests one representative case per family and removes a decisive measurement. Eleven positive cases use a fixture extension, eight use curated-runtime fixtures, and one uses intake. Passing those tests demonstrates that the front door classifies requests. The new local solver dispatches have separate executable tests. The method planner also requires an exact matching retained record to credit a parameter to the user, and requires comparable results on common inputs before allowing a method recommendation. **COUNT-1 is a protected representation on governed routes.** A declared corrected, imputed, normalized, or otherwise transformed layer cannot enter raw-count inference. Inherited AnnData `raw/X` still needs original-count provenance. Preparation separately stores raw counts, normalized expression, and embeddings. A bounded numeric check rejects negative, nonfinite, or fractional raw values. In an intake probe, `log2(1+x)` of original counts 1, 3, 7, 15 produced exact integers 1, 2, 3, 4. The HDF5 numeric check and bulk intake both accepted them under a false raw-count declaration. This was intake only. Source lineage and experimental records carry information that inspecting values or hashing a file cannot supply. Per-capture robust median/MAD checks, group-retention summaries and full-library totals are available. Mounted preparation checks totals against original counts and uses full-library totals or scran size factors for normalized expression. The primary count layer is preserved, and the downstream QC handoff binds the selected barcode axis, source digest and current receipt. Scrublet runs separately by declared capture. A single expected-rate setting defaults to 5% across captures. Fewer than 100 qualifying cells in a capture produces unavailable predictions there. Predictions are flags. The scDblFinder entry reports unavailable. EmptyDrops assesses whether unfiltered barcodes contain cells, and CellBender has a separate ambient-background route whose corrected matrix is quarantined from primary COUNT-1 inference. A clean matrix can still contain a condition-associated shift in ambient burden or disproportionate removal of a rare population. Reference-backed CellTypist or marker-panel labels and uncertain calls are available. Calibrated rare-type accuracy, stability to annotation/QC choices and held-out-donor performance still need measurement. ### Pseudobulk, bulk DE, batch protection and influential donors

The main differential-state path sums original counts by specimen, donor, condition and population. A treatment comparison within one population uses donor profiles. A paired comparison keeps donor blocks. Multilevel condition contrasts, explicit weights and nested likelihood-ratio tests are available for supported designs. Absent profiles are recorded separately from measured zeros, and profiles with fewer than 20 cells are flagged and retained. General staging can be called directly with an optional QC selection. The governed route binds a current mask. The paired-population design requires external label evidence, which the direct plugin staging request inspected here cannot itself supply. The lower-level preregistered route can consume it. These choices address the biological-replication problem demonstrated by Squair and Zimmerman. [Aggregation][pseudo] · [Population-label qualification][label-evidence] · [Plugin request][plugin-stage] · [Squair et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25960-2) · [Zimmerman et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21038-1)

The primary edgeR driver uses RLE library-size normalization for original counts and robust empirical-Bayes quasi-likelihood or an explicit LRT. It accepts fractional transcript estimates on a distinct tximport branch with aligned effective lengths and TMM-normalized gene-by-sample offsets. A separate bulk dream route fits a two-condition coefficient with a donor random intercept and voom precision weights for counts. Generalized single-cell pseudobulk implements independent or fully paired donor designs, with fixed donor blocking for pairs. Arbitrary longitudinal random slopes and cell-level mixed models extend beyond that route. Declared continuous expression has a Gaussian path. The protected-design svaseq and RUVg diagnostics can retain latent factors and rank diagnostics separately from the primary fit. A batch-corrected visualization does not enter the count likelihood. The mounted design checks declared donor identities, complete pairs where required, rank, residual degrees of freedom and conditioning. The relevant contrasts require at least three independent units per arm. Complete condition–batch confounding prevents identification of a separate condition effect. Dropping the batch column does not resolve that design problem. Three donors is an admission floor. A single-donor study can support description, and a study with many cells from three donors can still have poor power or be dominated by one donor. The simple PyDESeq2 Wald branch withholds Cook-flagged genes. A separate influence diagnostic evaluates **actual saved leave-one-donor or leave-one-pair fits** and records infeasible or missing omissions. Neither influence refits nor effect intervals accompany every primary count result. An edgeR robust fit or a low-cell warning alone cannot establish donor stability. ### Post-clustering inference, FDR and selection

The dedicated marker route implements negative-binomial count splitting. It uses known or independently sourced gene-specific dispersion, derives Leiden clusters on the discovery fold, then aggregates the inference fold by donor and cluster and fits a paired edgeR QL contrast. Promotion checks the exact split, clustering, aggregation and fit ancestry and requires a separate design, exposure, dispersion and calibration assessment. The synthetic mounted check exercised eight paired donors and 400 genes. Ordinary unsplit and simple Poisson-thinned rankings retain exploratory scores with inferential probabilities removed. ClusterDE has a proof interface. We located no mounted ClusterDE solver. Unreviewed same-cluster rankings should appear as descriptive markers. [Splitter][split] · [Paired marker fit][marker-fit] · [Ancestry and independent review][marker-review] · [Marker output rules][marker]

The two technical folds share donors. The mounted marker route does not require a separate holdout donor cohort. Such a cohort can test generalization, but assigning its validation labels from the same features later tested can reintroduce selection bias. A 60,000-observation NB probe illustrates the distributional assumption. With true size 4, supplied sizes 1, 4 and 16 gave between-fold correlations of −0.352, +0.005 and +0.353, respectively, despite exact count conservation. Measuring marker FDR would require repeated null studies with the full selection and testing procedure. The primary references for the requested comparison are Neufeld et al. in *Biostatistics*, their NB extension, and Gao, Bien & Witten’s selective test for hierarchical clustering in *JASA*. The latter has different clustering assumptions from Leiden. [Probe](reproduce_probes.py) · [Poisson count splitting](https://doi.org/10.1093/biostatistics/kxac047) · [NB extension](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12985) · [Selective inference](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.2022.2116331)

Public confirmatory gene rows are checked for finite `0 ≤ p ≤ q ≤ 1`. Governed multi-aim BH can freeze an exact ordered family before any member fit, count missing estimates in the arithmetic denominator using an internal value of one, and still emit null probabilities for those rows. The shared thesis-family path covers supported bulk contrasts. Individual solvers define their own eligible families. An analyst must report gene filtering, the denominator, and whether contrasts or populations were adjusted jointly. BH ordering is an arithmetic property. Frequentist FDR is an expectation over repeated studies and depends on valid input tests and dependence assumptions. scCODA posterior selection, native selection proofs and exploratory activity scores have distinct uncertainty semantics. [Joint BH][joint-bh] · [Synthesis validation][synthesis] · [BH under its stated assumptions](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1995.tb02031.x)

### Composition, neighborhood abundance, deconvolution and trajectories

Intrinsic transcriptional change and changing cell proportions answer different questions. The pseudobulk model tests a gene’s abundance within a chosen population across donors. scCODA instead consumes donor-by-cell-type **counts of captured, retained cells**, fits scCODA’s Bayesian Dirichlet–multinomial model with an explicit reference type, and selects relative effects by posterior inclusion. A change in capture efficiency, survival through QC, or the reference population can shift these fractions. Absolute cells per unit of tissue require an independent denominator. The current path rejects repeated donors and fewer than three donors per arm. Its formula contains condition indicators only. A batch column perfectly aligned with condition outside that input cannot be diagnosed by the composition fit. One NUTS chain and divergence checks give limited evidence about convergence. The receipt omits multi-chain R-hat, effective sample sizes, and effect intervals. Its expected false-discovery proportion is calculated from posterior probabilities of selected effects. [Count construction][comp-data] · [Support and selected-effect definition][comp] · [pertpy/NUTS execution][comp-fit] · [Büttner et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-27150-6)

Neighborhood differential abundance is **Milo-inspired**. It samples centers in a supplied KNN embedding, counts donors within overlapping neighborhoods, fits an NB Wald model, and reports ordinary BH and distance-weighted spatial FDR. The published Milo procedure uses refined sampling, TMM and edgeR quasi-likelihood. The implementation’s synthetic tests and chosen embedding cannot inherit the paper’s calibration. Its “spatial FDR” refers to neighborhoods in a cell-state graph. The graph need not encode physical tissue coordinates. Bulk deconvolution uses donor-reweighted nonnegative least squares on a matched reference. Reconstruction of the fitted genes is an in-sample check. An omitted cell type or mismatched RNA content can bias fractions without being detected by that check. [Neighborhood solver][neighborhood] · [Neighborhood tests][neighborhood-test] · [Bulk deconvolution][deconv] · [Dann et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-021-01033-z)

The donor trajectory route fits fixed cubic curves to normalized expression on a supplied common pseudotime axis. It checks independent or fully paired donors and compares donor-level curve shape. Confirmatory candidacy requires an independently supported axis bound to the feature family. Reusing an expression-derived axis in exploratory mode produces null p/q values. Unequal coverage of the curve affects precision, and the shape test assumes Gaussian donor errors with common covariance. The data may support a difference along a frozen lineage. Inferring topology, fate, direction of development or actual elapsed time requires further measurements. [Trajectory fit and estimand][trajectory] · [Independent-axis evidence][trajectory-proof] · [Differential trajectory analysis](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-44823-0)

### Pathways, backgrounds and regulatory networks

**Does the system enforce the exact tested expressed-gene universe? Yes on the governed DE-to-ORA route. Standalone enrichment tools have a different contract.** The public/synthetic plugin reopens the retained DE product, takes successfully estimated genes with finite ordered p/q, selects the ORA foreground using the frozen q threshold, and pins the resulting gene list. Tier A independently compares the ordered tested axis against the retained product. Gene-set membership is intersected with that axis before the size rule and hypergeometric test. The pathway correction retains failed eligible hypotheses in its denominator. This is the tested, estimable universe for that contrast. The number of genes in a genome, a database, or an input count matrix is an inadequate substitute. The validity of upstream filtering and gene-identifier mapping still matters. [Plugin derivation][path-plugin] · [Independent comparison][path-gate] · [ORA and eligible sets][ora] · [Wijesooriya et al.](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1009935)

Ranked fgsea uses a finite, complete ranking over that same eligible gene axis. There is no separate ORA-style hypergeometric background for GSEA. The plugin’s ranking is signed effect size, which can favor noisy large estimates. CAMERA uses the supplied expression/design and estimates inter-gene correlation and variance inflation. The decoupleR implementation runs ULM on a signed weighted reference and returns an exploratory activity score. ULM is one method in the decoupleR framework. A consensus activity estimate would require running and combining multiple methods. Each supports a different claim. Enrichment of selected genes, coordinated expression under a competitive null, and regulator-activity inference are different estimands. [R implementations][path-r] · [Wu & Smyth](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gks461) · [Badia-i-Mompel et al.](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioadv/vbac016)

The standalone regulon and motif tools trust a caller-declared background. With three selected genes, one reference set containing the same three, and an eight-gene background, p=q was 0.01786. Adding 992 unmeasured identifiers changed it to **6.02 × 10⁻⁹**. Both lists passed the shared validator. A DE-derived foreground therefore needs the governed path or independent proof of the background. Motif enrichment additionally requires explicit hit and no-hit calls. Lagged ridge-network edges describe associations from stage means or within-unit pairs. Reference membership, motif occurrence and correlation supply separate lines of evidence. None demonstrates physical binding, upstream causal control, or signaling in the measured specimen. [Standalone validation and tests][net-enrich] · [Network model and evidence labels][net] · [Probe](reproduce_probes.py)

### Batch integration, atlas transfer and spatial analysis

The mounted embedding route compares uncorrected PCA with Harmony or scVI. The NB scVI branch consumes checked counts. It checks graph identity/connectivity and neighborhood preservation. The geometry sample is bounded at 500 cells. The cross-batch edge fraction is descriptive. Biological silhouette needs independently attributed labels aligned to the same cells and is unavailable above 10,000 cells on this path. A geometric pass can coexist with unknown biological conservation. Conversely, an unchanged synthetic graph with two genuinely disjoint populations can fail its single-component rule. A separate notebook entry point uses a different governance default. Rare-population retention, conservation within cell type and held-out atlas mapping need their own evidence. [Mounted entry][embedding-entry] · [Embedding methods][embedding] · [Geometry and label checks][geometry] · [Pass rule][integration] · [Notebook entry][notebook] · [Luecken et al., scIB](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01336-8)

The spatial MCP solver accepts physical units and builds a symmetric KNN graph separately for each donor and tissue section. It computes Moran’s I, Geary’s C and cell-type colocalization using within-section permutations and distinct BH families. It also reports spatial autocorrelation of library size. This supports observations about the supplied sections. One section can run. The family’s implemented status applies to these prepared inputs. Independent donor-level treatment inference, an end-to-end imaging/registration workflow, a fitted spatial domain and spot deconvolution extend beyond this solver. Four simulated spots at coordinates 0, 1, 100, 101 in one section yielded three cross-gap edges among five edges with `k=2`. [Physical graph and null][spatial] · [Spatial result scope][spatial-result] · [Squidpy](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01358-2)

A full spatial-solver probe used two donors, one 12 × 12 section per donor and 32 equiprobable genes. Only capture depth varied, from 48 to 4,800 counts in opposite halves of each section. After log library-size normalization, **14/32 genes had Moran BH q < 0.05**, and **14/32 had Geary q < 0.05**. The library-size diagnostic detected the gradient (Moran’s I 0.916, q=0.005). The result still returned the gene statistics. This is one constructed failure of within-section exchangeability for a biological interpretation. Estimating spatial FDR requires repeated studies. In real tissue, depth itself can track biology, so the remedy should compare depth-conditioned and unadjusted analyses and report when they disagree. [Reproduction](reproduce_probes.py) · [Spatial normalization and warnings][spatial-result] · [Bhuva et al.](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-024-03241-7)

## Three prioritized computational additions

1. **Calibrate and repair donor-level inference before promoting confirmatory results.** Replace or qualify the pooled transcript-usage estimator against replicate-aware beta-binomial or Dirichlet-multinomial methods and an independent stage-wise implementation. For bulk and pseudobulk, compare the current edgeR QL, a prespecified voom/dream route where applicable, and an independent reference fit. Exercise paired and independent designs, unbalanced depth, small/moderate effects, donor leverage, sparse populations and confounding. Add effect intervals and whole-donor/pair omission summaries to confirmatory reports. **Acceptance** requires a new, independently held panel with predeclared nominal level, error ceiling, power floor, interval coverage and refusal rules. Report Monte Carlo intervals and every failed fit. Preserve the present failed panel as a regression case. [Frozen criteria][frozen-design] · [DTU comparator](https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.8900.2)
2. **Connect single-cell QC, label independence and donor-aware composition to the final claim.** Record a per-capture doublet/ambient disposition and differential retention by donor, condition and type. Bind count lineage and annotation origin across all supported public entry points. Evaluate marker FDR under misspecified dispersion and label assignment using independently measured cell identities or validated discovery/testing splits. Add adjusted or paired composition support, multi-chain diagnostics, credible intervals, reference-type sensitivity and leave-one-donor-out refits. Bind standalone DE-derived enrichment lists to the tested-universe receipt. **Acceptance** requires study-disjoint cell and nucleus cohorts, prespecified rare-type accuracy/abstention, differential-state error and power, composition interval coverage, and a recorded refusal for unidentifiable designs. [OSCA](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-019-0654-x) · [scCODA](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-27150-6) · [Count-splitting assumptions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12985)
3. **Build a replicate-aware spatial workflow and test the full agent on independent studies.** Add tissue-mask/radius-aware graphs, registered section geometry, depth and cell-mixture sensitivity, and donor-level comparisons across sections. Compare observed patterns with independent histology or cell labels, then score the operating agent from input through conclusion on blinded spatial, bulk and single-cell studies. Include plausible mislabels, fully confounded designs and absent assays. **Acceptance** requires prespecified spatial null error and power, sensitivity to graph choice and capture depth, disjoint donor/site replication, plus correct conclusions, false acceptances and false refusals with study-level uncertainty. Reproduce references and test numerical tolerances on both architectures. The repository names paired airway, paired PBMC stimulation and BICCN motor cortex nuclei as public reproduction candidates. A multi-donor spatial study with independently assessed histology would complete that evaluation panel. The five missing families still need their own measurements, implementations and validation. [Squidpy spatial graph methods](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-021-01358-2) · [scBench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09063) · [Repository evaluation proposal][evaluation]
